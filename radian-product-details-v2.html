When will it arrive?

You'll choose your exact delivery date and time slot at checkout. Inside Dhaka we offer 2-hour, same day and midnight delivery. All Bangladesh orders arrive in 1–3 days by trusted courier.

Can I send it anonymously or as a surprise?

Yes — many customers do. At checkout you can mark the order as an anonymous gift; leave the card unsigned or use initials, and our rider will simply say it's "a gift from someone who cares." We never reveal sender details.

What if the recipient isn't home?

Our rider calls the recipient discreetly on arrival. If unreachable, we coordinate with you immediately — we never leave a gift unattended at a door.
