None of the three felt right, and there is a reason

I was answering the wrong question. The question was never where do three more cards go. It is that the shop already says these things twice, and a fourth band of the same promises makes the page longer and the brand thinner, not stronger.

Look at them side by side. “freshness gurnaty” and “freshnes”. “2 hours delievry” and “very fast delivery in dhaka city”. Two masters, two screens, two places on the page — saying one thing. A customer reading both learns nothing the second time, and a brand that repeats itself sounds unsure.

So the fix is to give them different jobs

Trust badges are the glance. Delivery speed, freshness, the fact of a real shop — facts, three words each, read in half a second while deciding. They belong beside the price and nowhere else.

“Why buy from us” is the brand speaking. Not a fact — a reason. It should not look like another product widget in a white box; it should look like Radian, in Radian’s colours, with Radian’s name in it. That is the whole point of the section, and it is exactly what is missing today.

Three ways to do that — pick with the buttons above

D gives it a full-width band in the brand colour, straight after the buy box, and carries the Google rating into it as real proof. E puts three short proofs above the button, where the hesitation actually happens. F uses the empty space beside the photo, so the page grows by nothing at all.

In all three the trust badges shrink to a quiet service line under the buttons, so the same promise is never printed twice.
