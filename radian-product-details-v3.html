Before You Order

How fresh will it actually be?

When will it arrive?

You pick the exact date and time slot on the next step. Inside Dhaka: 2-hour, same day and midnight delivery. All Bangladesh: 1–3 days by trusted courier.

Can I send it anonymously or as a surprise?

Yes. At checkout you can mark it an anonymous gift — the card stays unsigned and our rider only says it's "a gift from someone who cares." We never reveal sender details.

What if the recipient isn't home?

Our rider calls the recipient discreetly on arrival. If unreachable, we coordinate with you immediately — we never leave a gift unattended at a door.

What if I'm not happy with it?

Send one photo within 24 hours. We replace it the same day, or refund you in full. That's the entire process.
