01How can I order flowers in Bangladesh?
Order fresh flowers online from Radian Flower & Gift Shop. We deliver bouquets and gift hampers across Bangladesh for birthdays, anniversaries, and special occasions.
02Does Radian Flower offer same-day delivery in Dhaka?
Yes. We provide same-day flower delivery in Dhaka and other major cities, ensuring fresh bouquets reach your doorstep on time.
03Can I customize my bouquet or gift hamper?
Yes. You can personalize bouquets, add custom messages, and choose themed gift hampers to make every gift special.
04What types of flowers and gifts are available?
We offer roses, lilies, carnations, gerberas, chocolate bouquets, balloons, and combo gift hampers suitable for birthdays, anniversaries, and festivals.
