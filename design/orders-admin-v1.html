Orders module — clean & bold (v1)

What changes, in one line: the pastel washes go, the words go, the numbers get big. White cards on a quiet ground, one coloured spine per tile, solid status pills, one bold action per row. Nothing on a screen that is not a fact or a button — every explanation moves behind the small ⓘ.

No tinted cardsWhite card, 1.5px border, a coloured spine on the left. Colour marks meaning; it never fills a box.
No loose proseNo eyebrow, no subtitle, no note paragraph at the bottom. Title + button. The why lives behind ⓘ.
Big numbers, bold textFraunces 800 for every figure, Jost 700 for labels, Manrope 800 for order numbers and nav. Nothing under 12.5px.
One stepper, solid pillsThe order page shows ONE journey, not three. Status is a solid pill you can read across the room.
