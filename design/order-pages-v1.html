The three pages after the order

Drawn at real size, in the shop's own fonts and colours — Spectral and Public Sans, brand purple, orchid, rose gold. Nothing here is a stock template.

What you asked for: the payment page says one thing and stops; the page right after an order is worth looking at, and its words and colour are what matter. So the success page is shown in two colour treatments — pick one and I will build it.

And no footer on any of these. The footer stays on the home page, category pages, product pages and the blog. Nowhere else.

What changes, in words

The pay page loses four lines of reassurance and keeps the order number, the amount and the button.
The success page loses the green “system success” tick and gets the shop's own colour, a headline that names the person receiving it, and one line that says when it arrives and where the messages go. The three cards under it stay but sit tighter.
Cash on delivery gets the amount to hand over in its own band, high on the page.
The footer is drawn only on the home page, category pages, product pages and the blog.
